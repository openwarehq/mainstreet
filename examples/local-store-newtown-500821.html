01 The shop

185 King Street

Local Store Newtown is a clothing shop at 185 King Street, on the Newtown end of the strip, open seven days from ten in the morning until six in the evening.

Those four facts are the whole of what the public map record holds, and the whole of what this page claims. What belongs here instead is the shop — what is on the rails, what gets held behind the counter, who brings it in.

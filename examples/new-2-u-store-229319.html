The shop

A clothing shop on King Street

New 2 U Store is at 377 King Street in Newtown, open every day of the week — eleven to six on weekdays, and from ten at the weekend.

That is the whole of what the public map record holds about it, and it is the whole of what this page claims. Everything else — the rails, the labels, what turns up on a Thursday — belongs to whoever runs the shop.

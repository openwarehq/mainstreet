Gents Cuts Barbers is a barber at 617 King Street, Newtown. The public map record lists the category, the street number, and the note above about appointments. There is nothing else in it.

So there is nothing else on this page. What belongs here is the part the shop knows: how to book, how long a cut takes, who is in on a Saturday.

What is missing

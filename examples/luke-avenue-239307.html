01 The bakery

Mornings only, six days a week

Luke Avenue is a bakery in Newtown. It opens at nine and closes at two, Monday through Saturday, and does not open on Sunday.

That is everything the public map record holds, and everything this page claims. What belongs here instead is the part only the bakery knows — what comes out of the oven, when, and what has usually gone by eleven.
